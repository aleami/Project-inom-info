About the Project

Navigating KTH Campus
made effortless

CampusFinder is a student built platform that helps KTH students locate buildings, book study rooms, and discover campus restaurants — all without the guesswork.

The Product

One platform for everything on campus

Finding a free study room between lectures, locating the right building for your exam, or discovering where lunch is served — these are daily pain points for KTH students. CampusFinder solves all of them in one place.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built by students, for students — every feature was designed around real campus workflows.

Interactive Map

Full KTH campus map with building markers, microwave locations, and a powerful search — built on Leaflet & OpenStreetMap.

Study Room Booking

Find and book group rooms across campus. The feature is currently in development — coming soon.

Restaurants & Cafés

All 6 campus dining spots in one place — La Campus, Syster O Bror, Brazilia, Nymble, 7-Eleven, and Murad Kiosken — with hours and menu links.

Smart Search

Find any building, room, or microwave by name.

Find Facilities

Locate microwaves, printers, and other campus facilities.

No Login Required

The entire platform is publicly accessible — no account, no barrier, just open and use.
